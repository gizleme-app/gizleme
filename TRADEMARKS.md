code — not the brand.

the domain **gizleme.com** are not licensed. They identify the service run at gizleme.com by its author and
nothing else.

You may:

- run, study, modify and redistribute the code under the AGPL;
- say that your project is "based on Gizleme" or "a fork of Gizleme".

You may not:

- call a modified or self-hosted version "Gizleme", or use a confusingly similar name;
- use the logo, word mark or icons for a service that is not gizleme.com;
- present your service as being operated by, or endorsed by, the author of Gizleme.

If you fork, pick your own name and artwork (the AGPL asks you to mark your changes anyway). Questions:
admin@gizleme.com.
