Forgot your password?

There is no reset — by design

Gizleme never asks for an email address or a phone number, so there is nothing we could send a reset link to. Your password is also the key to your encrypted contact backup: without it, that backup cannot be opened by anyone — including us.

If you're on a device where you're still logged in, go to Settings → Password → Change while you can. Otherwise, the only way forward is a new account: your friends can send you their invite links again.

Delete your account

This cannot be undone

Your username is released, your encrypted contact backup and all sessions are deleted from the server, and everything on this device is erased. Messages still waiting in the encrypted mailbox expire on their own within 14 days. Your contacts keep their own copies of past messages.
